How booking works

  1. Choose a city, dates and a car

    You'll only see cars at that branch that are free for the whole trip.

  2. Approve the M-Pesa prompt

    Choose where to collect the car, enter your details, and we send a payment request to your phone. Enter your PIN to pay.

  3. Get your confirmation

    Your screen updates the moment payment goes through, and we email your booking details.
