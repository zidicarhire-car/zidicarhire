What we collect

  • When you book: your name, M-Pesa phone number, email (if you give it), national ID or passport number, driving licence number and any pick-up notes.
  • When you pay: the M-Pesa receipt code and the result of the payment, which we receive from our payment provider PayHero. We never see your M-Pesa PIN.
  • When you chat or send a message: your name, the contact details you give and what you write.
  • On this device: your browser remembers your last city and the name, phone and email you typed, so you don't have to type them again. You can clear this in your browser settings.

Why we use it

To confirm and manage your hire, check who is collecting the car, send your receipt, contact you about your booking and keep records we are required to keep.

Who we share it with

PayHero and Safaricom (to process M-Pesa payments), our email provider (to send receipts) and our hosting provider. Owner to edit: add insurers or anyone else you share details with, for example after an accident.
